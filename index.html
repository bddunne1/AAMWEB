Our story

Six decades of doing it differently

In an industry of century-old companies, Aamstrand was founded on the idea that innovation and new technology belong in rope making.

1965

Aamstrand is founded and begins producing rope and twine in Bedford Park, Illinois.

1989

Operations expand and move to a larger facility in Manteno, Illinois.

2025

Sixty years of making rope in Illinois.

Today

Thirty lines of braided, twisted, twine and specialty cordage, shipped to customers nationwide.
