Most rope makers are more than a century old. We were founded on the idea that innovation and new technology belong in an industry set in its ways.

Our story

Made in Illinois

Aamstrand Ropes & Twines was founded in 1965. Aamstrand-brand products were made in Bedford Park, Illinois until 1989, when we expanded and moved to our current facility in Manteno, Illinois.

Today we make small twine, twisted rope, braided cord and double braided rope for commercial and industrial use. We also make rope for specialty work such as sailboat rigging, pleasure marine, climbing, rescue and tree care.
